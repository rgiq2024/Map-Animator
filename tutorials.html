How to use GIS One Map Animator

Step-by-step guides for the whole workflow, then every element in the Layers → Add panel — what it does, an example frame, its settings panel, how to place it, its animations and the settings you can change. Click a picture to see it full size.

Getting started

🚀 Start a project

  1. On the home page type a name and press Create, or press ✨ Auto video and describe the video in one sentence.
  2. You land in the editor. The six steps at the top are the workflow: Route · Layers · Look · Signs · Camera · Preview & export.
  3. Everything saves automatically in your browser. Use Save file (top right) to keep a .json copy, and Open file on the home page to open it again.

✏ Draw a route (optional)

  1. In 1 Route, click on the map to place points — the line curves through them by itself.
  2. Or press Use a route file for GPX, KML/KMZ, IGC or GeoJSON.
  3. Drag a point to move it, double-click a point for a sharp corner, click a point to pause there or split the leg.
  4. Legs (top right) set how you travel — walk, car, ferry, plane… each with its own symbol.
  5. No route needed? Press Skip and go straight to layers.

🧩 Add layers (elements)

  1. In 2 Layers → Add, open a section and click an element (all of them are explained below).
  2. Follow the hint at the top of the map: click a place, click along a line, or click around an area and press Finish.
  3. The new layer opens its settings on the left. Every layer has Timing & animation and When it shows: *Shows from*, *Animates for*, *Hides at*, *Stays until the end*, and Split at the playhead to show it only for part of the scene.
  4. Boxes, labels, legends and cards can be dragged on the video. Use Layers to show/hide, lock, reorder or delete.

🎨 Choose the look

  1. In 3 Look pick a Map theme (Classic, Dark, Satellite, Night…) and extras: place names, 3D mountains, globe, sky & clouds, 3D buildings.
  2. Set the route line colour, width and the travelling symbol.
  3. Each scene has its own look; a new scene copies the last one.

🪧 Add signs

  1. In 4 Signs press + Add sign, pick a style, click where it goes and type the text.
  2. Choose when it appears: when the line gets there, from the start, at the end or at a set time.

🎥 Set up the camera

  1. In 5 Camera choose a movement: Fixed view, Start to end, Keyframes, Rotate globe, Orbit or Follow the line.
  2. For keyframes: move the playhead, frame the view (drag, zoom, right-drag to rotate, tilt) and press ◆ Add keyframe at the playhead. Drag the diamonds on the timeline to retime them.
  3. Add start & end moves (zoom in from space / the country…) and lens effects (depth of field, vignette).

🎬 Scenes, transitions and the full preview

  1. Press + New scene in the top bar for another part of the story; each scene has its own route, layers, look and camera.
  2. In 6 Preview & export set To the next scene (dissolve, slide, zoom, wipe, circle, blur, flash…) and its length.
  3. Press All scenes in the timeline bar (or Shift+Space) to watch every scene together with the logo strip, ticker and sound running across them.

🏷 Logo strip, ticker and sound

  1. In 6 Preview & export → Logo & lower third: turn the logo strip on, replace it with your own wide PNG, and fill the lower third (country, place, date, ticker text separated by |).
  2. They run continuously across all scenes — the slide-in only happens at the very start.
  3. Sound is off by default: turn it on for automatic effects (explosions, engines, HUD beeps, typing) and a background score; add music or record a voiceover.

📤 Preview and export the video

  1. Pick a Preset (YouTube 1080p/4K, Reels 9:16, Instagram 4:5, Square, Cinema 21:9…) or set resolution and frame rate.
  2. Press Render — the MP4 is made in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Keep the tab open while it renders.
  3. Save this frame as PNG gives a still for a thumbnail.

✨ Auto video from one sentence

  1. Press ✨ Auto video and describe it: places, date and what happened (e.g. “Train collision at Urda on the Madrid–Seville line, 28 October 2026”).
  2. Press Find the places, check the plan, pick the building blocks (route A→B, ripples, before/after images, weather, ticker, basemap, music).
  3. Press Create the video — you get editable scenes you can change like any other project.
