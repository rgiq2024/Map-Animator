How to draw
  1. Click to place points. The line curves smoothly through them by itself.
  2. Click on the line to add a point in between.
  3. Double-click a point to make it a sharp corner with straight lines. Double-click again to curve it.
  4. Drag a point to move it.
  5. Click a point for more: pause here, split the leg, or delete it.
  6. Legs (top right) set how you travel: walk, car, ferry, plane… Add a new leg to switch.

                How to make a map video

                1. Route. Click on the map to place points, or load a GPX / KML / KMZ / IGC file. The line curves smoothly through your points. Click the line to add a point, double-click a point for a sharp corner, drag points to move them, and click a point to pause there or split the leg.
                2. Legs. Each leg has a way of travelling — walk, car, train, ferry, plane and more — with its own symbol and line style. Use + New leg to switch.
                3. Layers. Draw or import boundaries, areas, floods, lines, points, animated symbols, text and captions. Each has its own animation and timing — trace a boundary, spread a flood from a point, raise water over real terrain, pulse a symbol, type out a caption.
                4. Look. Pick a map theme, place names, 3D mountains, line colour and symbol.
                5. Signs. Add boards, flags, photos and more. They pop up when the line reaches them.
                6. Camera. Film a fixed view, glide from a start view to an end view, or follow the line.
                7. Preview & export. Set the stills and resolution, then render an MP4 right in your browser.

                Use scenes for separate parts of a trip; they are joined into one video. Keyboard: Space play/pause, Ctrl+Z undo, Delete remove the selected point or sign.

                FAQ

                Is it free? Do I need an account?

                Yes, it's free, and there's no account. Your projects are saved in this browser only.

                Where does my video get made?

                In your browser, frame by frame. Nothing is uploaded. Keep the tab open while it renders.

                Which browsers work?

                Recent Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox. Chrome and Edge make H.264 MP4s that play everywhere.

                How do I move a project to another computer?

                Use Save file in the editor, then open the file on the other computer.

                Can I use the videos commercially?

                Yes. Keep the map attribution that is drawn in the corner of the video.

                About

                Map Animator is an open-source tool for making animated travel maps. It runs entirely in your browser using MapLibre GL, OpenStreetMap data via OpenFreeMap, Esri imagery, AWS terrain tiles, Nominatim search and FOSSGIS OSRM routing.

                Source code: github.com/rgiq2024/Map-Animator
